# TikTok Script

TikTok is the purest **discovery engine** in social: the For You Page decides reach from how
people respond to the video, not from your follower count.

1. **The hook is brutal — ~1 second.** Stop the scroll instantly on three channels.
2. **Watch-time and rewatches are everything.** Design for completion and a loop.
3. **Make TikToks, not ads.** Native, authentic, slightly raw content beats polished ads.
4. **Sound and trends are part of discovery** — ride them where they fit.
5. **TikTok is also a search engine** — keywords in spoken, on-screen and caption.

## Step 0 — Read the foundation first

Load `brand-profile.md` and `voice.md`.

## Step 1 — Lock the concept, goal, and format

- **One idea.**
- **Goal** — reach, saves, follows, or comments.
- **Format** — talking head, storytime, POV, tutorial, listicle, green-screen, Stitch/Duet, faceless.

## Step 2 — Decide the sound

Ride a trending sound only where it genuinely fits; otherwise original audio or a safe music bed.

## Step 3 — Write the 1-second hook

Use `hook-writer`. Fire on all three channels at once. No intro, no logo, no "hey guys".

## Step 4 — Structure for watch-time and the loop

Front-load value, keep an open loop, cut dead air, design a loop or rewatch trigger.

## Step 5 — Write the production doc

Output a shootable doc: shot · on-screen text · spoken · sound · timing.

## Step 6 — TikTok SEO + caption + hashtags

Place keywords in spoken script, on-screen text, and caption. Hand caption to `caption-writer`.

## Step 7 — Production: film-first, or the Veo pack

Default to native filming for faces/voices. For faceless/b-roll use the Veo prompt pack.

## Step 8 — Hand off

- **Caption →** `caption-writer`.
- **Schedule →** `scheduling-and-queue`.

## Quality bar — self-check

- Hook in ~1 second on all three channels.
- Built for watch-time with a loop/rewatch trigger.
- Sound chosen intentionally.
- Native, not ad-like.
- TikTok-search keywords in spoken + on-screen + caption.
- Output is a shootable production doc.

## Edge cases & pushback

- **"Make it a polished ad"** → push back; ad-style underperforms.
- **"Put a trending dance sound on our B2B explainer"** → don't force it.
- **"Generate it with AI instead of filming"** → fine for faceless/b-roll; filming often wins.
- **Thin concept** → shape the one idea; don't pad.
- **Repurposing a Reel** → re-fit for TikTok, don't cross-post a watermarked file.
