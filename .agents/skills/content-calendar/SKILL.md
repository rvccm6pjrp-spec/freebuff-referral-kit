# Content Calendar

A content calendar isn't a blank grid you fill by heroics — it's a **sustainable operating
rhythm**: a recurring structure that generates posts at a cadence you can actually hold.

1. **Cadence equals capacity.** A sustainable floor beats an ambitious plan abandoned in February.
2. **A recurring skeleton beats a blank page.** Define buckets and named series, then fill in.

## Step 0 — Read the foundation first

Load `social-strategy.md`, `content-pillars.md`, and `brand-profile.md`.

## Step 1 — Size the cadence to real capacity

Estimate honest capacity and set a sustainable cadence per platform. Pick one or two primary
platforms to do well. Use repurposing to cover more without proportionally more work.

## Step 2 — Build the recurring skeleton

Map **buckets** — pillar × format × slot/day/platform — so each slot is a standing instruction.

## Step 3 — Design one or two recurring series

Add a named franchise in a fixed slot for anticipation and recognition. One or two, not every slot.

## Step 4 — Anchor dates + reserve reactive room

Layer time-bound anchors and reserve ~20–30% for reactive/timely content.

## Step 5 — Set the operating rhythm

Define a planning ritual, batch creation, and a buffer/queue so a bad week doesn't break the streak.

## Step 6 — Deliver + hand off

Output cadence + skeleton + series + planned/reactive split + anchors, plus a filled sample week.

- **Fill a specific period →** `batch-content-plan`.
- **Write each piece →** the content skills.
- **Schedule →** `scheduling-and-queue`.

## Quality bar — self-check

- Cadence sized to capacity, per platform.
- Recurring skeleton of buckets, not a one-off list.
- One or two named series.
- Reactive room reserved alongside anchors.
- Planning rhythm + buffer, not just a grid.
- Hand-offs clear.
- Multi-platform made feasible by repurposing.

## Edge cases & pushback

- **Overcommitting** → right-size hard to a sustainable floor.
- **Too rigid** → reserve reactive slots.
- **"Just give me 20 post ideas"** → that's batch-content-plan.
- **"Build it around our best times"** → lifetime analytics aren't a best-time model.
- **No pillars/strategy yet** → derive provisional pillars.
- **Keeps abandoning schedules** → fix the rhythm, not just the grid.
