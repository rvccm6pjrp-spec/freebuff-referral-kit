# Reels Script

Reels are Instagram's **discovery engine**: most reach comes from non-followers, decided by how
viewers respond.

1. **The hook window is ~3 seconds.** Layer verbal + on-screen text + visual.
2. **Watch-through and sends rule.** Sends-per-reach are weighted more than likes.
3. **Write the muted version first.** If on-screen text doesn't carry the story, it fails.
4. **Eligibility gates come before ranking:** original, no watermarks, has audio, clear niche.
5. **The Reel also lives on your grid** — plan the cover frame.

## Step 0 — Read the foundation first

Load `brand-profile.md` and `voice.md`.

## Step 1 — Lock the concept, goal, and length tier

- **One idea.**
- **Goal** — reach, sends, saves, follows, or comments.
- **Length** — ~15–35s is the sweet spot; up to ~90s for tutorials.

## Step 2 — Decide the audio (and write sound-off anyway)

Original audio for most founder/brand content; trending audio only where it fits. Trending audio
is added natively in-app. The Reel must work on mute.

## Step 3 — Write the 3-second hook

Use `hook-writer`. Fire on all three channels. The on-screen text must work silently.

## Step 4 — Structure for watch-through and sends

Front-load value, open a loop early, change the visual every 2–4 seconds, engineer a loop. Then ask:
**who would DM this to whom, and why?**

## Step 5 — Write the production doc + cover brief

Output a shootable doc: shot · on-screen text · spoken · audio · timing, plus a cover-frame brief.

## Step 6 — Instagram SEO + caption + hashtags

Keyword in the first 3 seconds spoken, on-screen text, and caption's first sentence.

## Step 7 — Test with Trial Reels; produce film-first or with the Veo pack

For unproven hooks, recommend a Trial Reel. Default to native filming. For faceless/b-roll use Veo.

## Step 8 — Hand off

- **Caption →** `caption-writer`.
- **Schedule →** `scheduling-and-queue`.

## Quality bar — self-check

- Hook in ~3 seconds on all three channels.
- Works on mute.
- Built for watch-through with a loop.
- Can name who sends this to whom.
- Audio choice intentional.
- Clears eligibility gates.
- Keywords in spoken + on-screen + caption.
- Cover-frame brief included.

## Edge cases & pushback

- **"Just repost my TikToks"** → watermark + originality gates throttle reach; re-cut clean.
- **"Add the trending audio for me"** → human adds IG's licensed audio in-app.
- **"Make it a polished brand ad"** → authentic, person-first content travels further.
- **"Guarantee this goes viral"** → no guarantees; a Trial Reel tests it.
- **Thin concept** → shape the one idea; don't pad.
