# Community Engagement

Comments are not a vanity metric. They are the place where a viewer decides whether you are a
person or a link dispenser. The rule is simple: **be useful first, mention the link only when
asked or when it is the actual answer.**

## Daily routine (15 minutes)

1. **First 2 hours after posting:** reply to every substantive comment. This is when the
   algorithm and the humans are both watching.
2. **Next day:** one follow-up reply per thread, not a new comment bumping the post.
3. **Week end:** pin the best comment (a question with a useful answer) or a text comment with
   the link placement.
4. **Log** repeated questions; they become the next post.

## Reply patterns

**Question about the product**

> Sì, è gratis e non chiede la carta. Se vuoi provarlo, il link è in bio; se lo usi ricevo
> sessioni extra. ADV.

**"Link?"**

> È in bio. Te lo incollo qui solo se non lo trovi: [link]. ADV: se lo usi ricevo sessioni
> extra.

**Skeptic / "è spam?"**

> Capisco. È un referral: se passi dal mio link ricevo un beneficio, per te il prezzo non
> cambia. Il prodotto puoi provarlo anche senza. Se ti va, ti dico cosa ho testato.

**Criticism that is right**

> Hai ragione su X. Ho aggiornato il video/la caption. Grazie.

**Hostile**

One neutral reply, then stop. Hide, do not engage, do not ask the audience to defend you.

## DM policy

- **Never initiate** a DM to send a link.
- Reply to inbound DMs with the same disclosure.
- Do not send identical messages to multiple people. Personalize the first line or do not send.
- If someone asks for help, answer the question before the link.
- Move nothing to WhatsApp/Telegram to bypass platform rules.

## Moderation

- Hide spam and scam replies; ban repeat offenders.
- Do not delete honest negative comments. Reply once if it adds context.
- Do not buy comments, likes or engagement pods.
- If a comment contains a threat or personal data, report it and do not republish it.

## What to measure

- **Replies per post** and **questions asked** (demand signal).
- **Saves and shares**, not likes.
- **Clicks from the bio** after a comment spike, to see if the conversation moved.
- **Signups you can trace**; ask "come ci hai trovato?" only when it is natural.

## Output

- A reply plan for the first 10 comments, with three templates adapted to the post.
- A pinned-comment text.
- One sentence on what the comments revealed about the audience.
